How it works

  1. 01

    Hand it to your coding agent

    Claude Code, Codex, or anything that takes a prompt. It clones the repo, walks you through a small Slack app, and starts the bird in a dry run.

  2. 02

    The bird answers for you

    Direct messages only. It waits 25 to 90 seconds, then replies as you. It never agrees to a deadline, a meeting or a decision.

  3. 03

    It knows when to stop

    Urgent messages, call requests and “are you a bot?” get no reply. The bird rings a bell on your laptop instead. If you type in a conversation yourself, it backs off.
